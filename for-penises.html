What is a penis toy?
A penis toy is designed to make masturbation and partnered play more pleasurable and exciting. These toys come in many shapes and styles, each offering a unique way to enhance stimulation. Vibrating masturbators deliver targeted sensations, strokers create a realistic, textured feel, and cock rings help maintain stronger, longer-lasting erections. Choose body-safe materials such as silicone, and always use plenty of water-based lubricant to maximise comfort.
