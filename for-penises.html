What is a penis toy?
A penis toy is designed to make masturbation and partnered play more pleasurable and exciting. These toys come in many shapes and styles, each offering a unique way to enhance stimulation. Vibrating masturbators deliver targeted sensations, strokers create a realistic, textured feel, and cock rings help maintain stronger, longer-lasting erections. Whether used solo or with a partner, penis toys allow you to explore new kinds of pleasure and discover what feels best for your body.
What types of penis toys are there?

Most penis sex toys are made from body-safe silicone and feature a variety of textures, intensities, and designs.
What makes a good first time penis toy?

Choose body-safe materials such as silicone, and always use plenty of water-based lubricant to maximise comfort.
